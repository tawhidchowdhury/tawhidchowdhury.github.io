02 / Research Thesis

THESISFebruary 2026

Adaptive Urban Traffic Control via Reinforcement Learning

Python · SUMO · Q-learning · Deep Q-Networks

I formulated adaptive signal control as a sequential decision problem and modeled a traffic intersection in Dhaka using the SUMO simulator. The study compared tabular Q-learning and DQN with fixed-time signal heuristics under normal and disrupted traffic conditions.

In the simulated evaluation, the RL controllers reduced average waiting time and improved throughput relative to fixed-time control. The thesis was successfully defended and approved by the BRAC University Thesis Committee; a manuscript is in preparation.

Thesis group leader · Supervisors: Prof. Amitabha Chakrabarty and Prof. Md. Golam Rabiul AlamRead the thesis ↗
